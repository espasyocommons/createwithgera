01 — About Me

A designer who can also build the thing.

I'm Gera — a UX/UI designer and product builder based in the Philippines. I care about the parts that make a product actually make sense: the user flows, the structure, the small decisions most people never notice but always feel.

What sets me apart is that I don't just design screens — I build them. My design-and-development background means my work is polished, practical, and genuinely dev-friendly, so ideas make it from Figma into something real without losing their intent.

I've taken a product from research all the way to a live launch on my own — OurSpace, a writing studio that reached 1,000+ users in its first month. I bring that same end-to-end thinking to every project, whether it's a landing page or a full app.
